Hampi Heritage Yatra

Explore the timeless ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire

Virupaksha Temple

The oldest functional temple in Hampi, dedicated to Lord Shiva. Known for its towering Gopuram and intricate carvings.

Vittala Temple

Famous for its Stone Chariot and musical pillars. It represents the pinnacle of Vijayanagara temple architecture.

Achyutaraya Temple

Located between Matanga Hill and Gandhamadana, this temple features a grand courtyard and beautiful carvings.

Hazara Rama Temple

The private temple of the Kings, adorned with thousands of carvings depicting the Ramayana.

Krishna Temple

Built by Krishnadevaraya to celebrate his victory over Udayagiri, known for its elegant carvings of Balakrishna.

Badavi Linga

The largest monolithic Shiva Linga in Hampi, permanently surrounded by water in its sanctum.

Sasivekalu Ganesha

A giant monolithic statue of Lord Ganesha, named after the 'mustard seed' shape of the belly.

Kadalekalu Ganesha

One of the largest Ganesha statues in South India, situated on the slopes of Hemakuta Hill.

Pattabhirama Temple

A sprawling temple complex known for its serene atmosphere and vast pillared halls.

Chandrakeshavara Temple

A beautiful temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu, showcasing the detailed artistry of the era.

Royal Enclosure

The seat of power, containing the remains of palaces, administrative buildings, and water systems.

Lotus Mahal

A secular building featuring Indo-Islamic architecture, shaped like a lotus bud.

Elephant Stables

A grand structure with 11 domed chambers used to house the royal elephants.

Queen's Bath

An ornate bathing chamber for the royal ladies, featuring Islamic-style arches and balconies.

Mahanavami Dibba

A massive stone platform used for royal ceremonies during the Navaratri festival.

Hampi Bazar

The ancient marketplace stretching in front of the Virupaksha Temple, once a center for global trade.

Virupaksha Bazar

A double-storied colonnade that housed merchants and travelers in the 15th century.

Tungabhadra River

The lifeline of Hampi, famous for its coracle boat rides and boulder-strewn landscapes.

Stepped Tank

An architectural marvel made of black schist, used for ritual bathing by the royals.

Pushkarani

Holy water tanks attached to temples, used for festivals and religious purification.

Ancient Aqueducts

A sophisticated water management system that supplied water to the entire city.

Underground Shiva Temple

A temple built several feet below ground level, often filled with water during the monsoon.

Watch Towers

Strategically placed towers used by guards to keep watch over the royal palace grounds.
