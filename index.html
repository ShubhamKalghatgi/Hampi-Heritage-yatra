Comprehensive Temple Repository

Virupaksha Temple

The 7th-century spiritual anchor of Hampi, dedicated to Lord Shiva. Its 50-meter gopuram is a masterpiece of Dravidian engineering.

Vittala Temple Complex

Home to the iconic Stone Chariot and 56 musical pillars that resonate with different notes when tapped gently.

Lotus Mahal

A secular pavilion featuring a sophisticated natural air-cooling system using water channels within the walls.

Hazara Rama Temple

The king's private temple. The outer walls depict 1,000 bas-reliefs of the Ramayana in narrative sequence.

Elephant Stables

An imposing structure with 11 domed chambers, reflecting a fusion of Islamic and Hindu architectural motifs.

Sasivekalu Ganesha

A monolithic 8-foot statue of Ganesha carved from a single boulder. 'Sasivekalu' means mustard seed in Kannada.
